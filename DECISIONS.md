# Build decisions (for India's review)

Judgement calls made while building from `specification.md`. Spec §12 decisions are unchanged. Each line below is open to reversal.

## 2026-11-15
1. **Layer 3 is pending, not estimated.** Sayari was not connected in the build session, so every Layer 3 component (readout, flow volumes, exposure curves, exporter table) renders an explicit "awaiting Sayari snapshot" state. No illustrative numbers are shown as data.
2. **Simulation runs on labelled placeholders.** The flagged share at each threshold, and most input ranges, are structural placeholders with a visible "placeholder" badge and a "scenario, not forecast" banner. The release rate is calibrated from CBP once Layer 2 is captured.
3. **Simulation threshold ladder.** The dial space is collapsed into six cumulative steps for the threshold curve: T0 listed → T1 direct (≈ current) → T2 tiers 2–3 → T3 tiers 2–4 product → T4 + one-hop ownership → T5 + research-named. This order is my choice; the spec doesn't fix one.
4. **Hidden exposure scales with depth.** Untraced exposure is surfaced in proportion to rule depth (0, .25, .5, .75, .9, 1 for T0…T5). This is a structural assumption, shown in the Model box.
5. **Shortage duration model.** shortage = max(0, ramp + gap/capacity − buffer×100/gap). It's simple and stated on the page.
6. **The copula couples only hidden exposure and rerouting** (ρ = 0.5, stated assumption), as in the spec's correlation example.
7. **Layer 2 solar proxy.** HTS 8541 is used if CBP figures at that level are found (the 2026 dashboard has HTS-4 filters). Otherwise Electronics. Both are labelled as upper bounds; HTS 8541 also covers LEDs, diodes and other semiconductors.
8. **R1 research-named flags take their tier from the factor name** (e.g. `forced_labor_aspi_origin_subtier` counts at E2 under the research scope), so the tier-depth and trigger-scope dials compose.
9. **Corroboration moves confidence by one named level** (e.g. medium → high), per spec §3.
10. **Flow diagram.** The last column is "left on the US market if flagged supply is removed", drawn at absolute scale so the drop is visible. The lavender band is supply flagged only at deeper settings: the shadow market itself.
11. **Design.** Taken from the reference screenshots: indigo/periwinkle striped hero, ice-blue panels, Inter Tight display (tight tracking) with Source Sans 3 body. No mono or condensed fonts. Exposure colours were checked with the dataviz palette validator (A1/R1 pass with labels as secondary encoding).
12. **Separate git repo.** `git init` was run inside the project folder because the home directory is itself a repo (remote EvacSimulator). Remote: github.com/indiaclarke03-ops/Shadow-Market.

## 2026-11-16
13. **Release rate by value, not count.** The Oct 2026 CBP dashboard's all-industry line counts (15,547 released) are lower than the Nov 2025 guide's (39,829). Counts cannot fall, so the definition changed. Values agree across both cuts, so `build.py` computes released ÷ (released + denied) by value (HTS 8541: 79.5%) and shows the count rate (65.4%) as secondary. See `pipeline/notes/layer2_research.md`.
14. **C-018 resolved.** Xinte Energy is not named in the Aug 2026 Entity List notice (L2-032) or on the list page (L2-022). The claim now says so, with those sources added; confidence stays medium.
15. **Layer 3 is measured in shipments, not dollars.** Sayari's US import records are bills of lading with weight but no declared value (raw: `search_shipments_first-solar_…`). Market shares, curves and cliffs use counts of US-bound shipment records for HS 8541.42/.43 over the 730-day window. Tolerated exposure (Layer 3 − CBP USD) is shown as "—" until there is a USD measure. The value-basis dial's first position is relabelled "Share of US imports".
16. **`psa_` factors are excluded.** "Possibly the Same As" factors are inherited from a record that may not be the same entity. WRO-origin and ASPI-adjacent factors are outside the UFLPA dials and are listed by `build.py` as unmapped.
17. **Freight forwarders count as untraced.** When the shipper of record is a logistics firm (by name pattern in `extract_sayari.py`, 259 entities, 6.7% of shipments), its risk factors describe the forwarder's network, not the goods. Exporters with no inbound trade in Sayari (1.9%) are also untraced. Traced share: 91.3%.
18. **Inline responses saved abridged.** Responses too large for context were copied byte-for-byte from disk. Smaller responses returned inline were saved with every data field and an `abridged` block listing the boilerplate left out (guidance text, risk-factor definitions). Universe pulls are the top 600 module and 300 cell suppliers. The unpulled tails (1,293 module suppliers with ≤5 shipments each, i.e. ≤6,465 shipments, ≤4%; 46 cell suppliers with 1 each) are excluded from the denominator.
19. **Upstream columns come from hub-inbound rollups, not per-exporter tracing.** `get_upstream_supply_chain` truncated HS 2804.61 to 2804 (industrial gases) and errored with a component filter. S1/S2 in `tiers.json` therefore split polysilicon and wafer shipments into the hubs (VNM, IND, IDN, MYS, THA) by the exporting entity's own flags at the default dial setting, weighted by each hub's share of US module shipments. Limitations: HS 3818/2804.61 into IDN include semiconductor-grade material (SUMCO, Hanna Instruments), and Chinese PV majors' own upstream inside China is invisible to cross-border trade data.
20. **Country of exporter.** Taken in this order: legal-form suffix in the name (SDN BHD, PT, Công ty, PTE LTD …) → registry identifier prefix → first non-US country. One manual override (Hanwha Solutions → KOR) is in `pipeline/country_overrides.csv` with its reason.
21. **M7 spot checks substitute entity summaries for `get_record`.** Profiles and summaries expose source names but no record ids, so `get_record` could not be sampled. Six exporters were checked against live summaries: 5 matched the rollup flags exactly, and Waaree's profile lacked its E2 flags (corroboration −1). VSUN (C-022, detention ended in denial) gets +1. Qcells (C-021) gets no uplift because the detained cells were cleared.
22. **C-013 is no longer held.** The intent rule released it automatically because Layer 3 shows a cliff on `trigger_scope` (0% → 13.3% at research-named). The cliff is real but small. India should decide whether the claim's wording still fits.
