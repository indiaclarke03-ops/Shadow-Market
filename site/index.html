US solar imports × the Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act

0% flagged.84% linked.

The US bans goods made with forced labour in Xinjiang, China. Check only the companies that ship solar cells and panels to America, roughly how the rule is applied today, and almost nothing is flagged. Follow the same supply chains back to the polysilicon and wafers, and most shipments connect to companies linked to forced labour.

This tool measures that gap, the shadow market: supply the law is meant to cover but doesn’t reach. It shows where the gap sits, why the rule stops where it does, and what would happen to US solar supply if the rule went further.

    Simulation · 10,000 possible futures

    What if customs
    looked further?

    Suppose US customs started blocking solar shipments whose supply chains link to forced labour further upstream. How much solar supply would America lose, and would there be a shortage? Nobody knows the inputs exactly, so the simulation plays the scenario out 10,000 times, each with slightly different assumptions, and shows the range of what could happen.
